Color bar title options

Mock-ups of four ways to title a color bar, drawn from the catalog baseline colored by temperature. Only the bar and its title change.

A. Title above the bar, left-aligned

Written horizontally above a vertical bar, aligned with its left edge, the bar starting below it: ggplot2's default for a vertical colour bar. Reads like a legend's title; a long title takes width from the plot.

B. Title above the bar, centred

The same, centred over the bar. Neat with a short title, but a title wider than the bar runs over the plot's edge, as here.

C. Title along the bar (current)

Rotated to read upward beside the bar, like a y-axis title: matplotlib's default. Compact, but you turn your head to read it.

D. Horizontal bar below the plot

A horizontal bar under the x-axis title, its title above it on the left: common in maps and dashboards (Datawrapper, Flourish). Frees the plot's width; costs height.

Implemented: legend.color_bar.layout

legend.color_bar.layout = title_above (A, default)

legend.color_bar.layout = title_along (C)

legend.color_bar.layout = below (D)
