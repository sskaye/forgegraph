Default font comparison

The catalog baseline drawn in each candidate. Every font is open-licensed (SIL Open Font License) and has the light, regular, medium, semibold and bold weights a theme can use. Arial and Helvetica, which some journals require, can't be bundled; their open metric-compatible clones have only regular and bold.

Source Sans 3

Adobe's humanist sans; the default until now. Open shapes, compact width.

IBM Plex Sans

IBM's grotesque with engineered, technical details; distinct 0 and O.

Inter

A neo-grotesque designed for screens, close to Helvetica and Arial in feel; tall x-height.

Roboto

Google's neo-grotesque (Android's system font); neutral and slightly condensed. Now the default.

Open Sans

A humanist sans with very open shapes; often recommended for small chart labels.

Noto Sans

Google's family covering almost every script; related to Open Sans.

Fira Sans

Mozilla's humanist sans, designed to stay legible at small sizes.

Lato

A humanist sans with geometric touches; warm, rounded.

Libre Franklin

A revival of Franklin Gothic, the classic of newspaper and infographic text.

Public Sans

The U.S. government's design-system font: plain and neutral.

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

The Braille Institute's font for low vision: every character made distinct.

IBM Plex Serif

A serif for comparison (bundled already).

Source Serif 4

Adobe's serif, partner to Source Sans; a serif for comparison.
